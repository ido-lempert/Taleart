# Digest: Storyly "What's new" (round 3, current page)
accessed: 2026-10-20 | source: https://www.storyly.io/whats-new-in-storyly (newest entry Oct 12, 2026) plus detail pages /news/... | publisher: Storyly

## Analytics (most relevant to TaleArt)
- Oct 12, 2026 — Analytics Upgrade. Widget Performance (per widget) is reported separately from Content Performance (per story group, story, video). Widget types with their own analytics: Banners, Swipe Cards, Canvas, with breakdown per slide, card, grid, and item. Metrics named and defined consistently across widget types: Reach, Impression, Click, Engagement Rate, CTR. Engagement Rate divides by people who saw the widget. Active Users moved to Settings. New analytics data starts July 25, 2026. Full metric definitions are NOT on the public page; customers are pointed to their customer success manager. Confidence: high (names); definitions not public.
- Earlier Story Bar and Video Feed metrics were renamed: Content Reach, Content Impression, Content Action Click.
- May 4, 2026 — Retail Media integration with retailmediatools: Story Bar as a sponsored ad unit; campaigns and metrics tracked in the retailmediatools dashboard. Detail page returned 404. Relevance: a metered metric living in a third-party system.

## Audiences
- Sep 24, 2026 — Audiences page: Braze sync type per audience (daily vs one-time sync); Bloomreach change counts (users added and removed since last trigger); Bloomreach audiences renamable.
